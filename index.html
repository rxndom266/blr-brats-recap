How the group runs

Patterns that showed up across the ten days.

  • The organisers run weekend meetups and post event posters. Members are encouraged to meet outside the chat and to plan weekday meetups themselves. Weekend meetups are paused for now.
  • Requests to join a separate event group go to the organiser by direct message, not in the main chat.
  • Self-promotion needs a heads-up to the admins first.
  • The admins delete messages and remove members from time to time. On 12 Nov the organiser said jokes at women’s expense and baiting were the reason. The export does not show the deleted text.
  • Newcomers are asked to introduce themselves. Several asked for message history to catch up, which is why recaps keep coming up.
  • Members used polls to map where everyone lives and how old they are, to plan drives and meetups.
